Assignment 5 · Research proposal (LaTeX)

Plan how you would investigate your research question. 2–3 pages + references.

  1. Background – what is known, why it matters
  2. Research question(s) – 1–2, precise
  3. Method – sources, data, analysis
  4. Work plan – timeline / Gantt with milestones
  5. Risks – small risk table with mitigation
  6. References – 5+, with BibTeX

Submit PDF + .tex/.bib (zip) as G<group>_<Lastname>_A5 · work time in Lab 12 (Mon 14.12)

Assignment 6 · Literature review

Survey and critically compare what is known about your research question. 3–4 pages + references.

  1. Scope & search – where and how you searched
  2. Synthesis by 2–4 themes – not paper by paper
  3. Comparison table of key sources
  4. Gaps & open questions
  5. Conclusion – what it means for your poster section
  6. 8+ sources (3+ peer-reviewed, at least half found by you), each with DOI or link

Submit PDF as G<group>_<Lastname>_A6 · work time in Lab 12 (Mon 14.12)

⚠️
Individual work. AI tools only for language help, declared in a footnote. Every reference must be verifiable – invented or unchecked sources lose points.
